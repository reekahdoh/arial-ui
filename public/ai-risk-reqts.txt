
# AI Solution Risk Evaluation System (AI-RES)

## 1. Purpose

The system will assess, score, and continuously monitor the **organizational, legal, technical, operational, and ethical risk** of companies that are considering whether to:

* **Buy** an AI solution (vendor product, SaaS, API)
* **Build** an AI solution in-house
* **Use** an AI capability inside an existing platform

The system must produce:

* A **quantified AI Risk Score**
* A **risk classification** (Low / Moderate / High / Critical)
* A **risk profile report** suitable for legal, compliance, security, and executive review
* A **go/no-go recommendation** with mitigation actions

---

## 2. Core Principles

The system must be designed around these realities:

1. AI behavior is **non-deterministic**
2. Risk comes primarily from **data, not code**
3. AI risk **changes over time** (model drift, regulation, misuse)
4. Vendor claims are **not trusted inputs** without verification
5. AI risk is **context dependent** (same model, different use = different risk)

---

## 3. Risk Domains the System Must Evaluate

### 3.1 Legal & Regulatory Risk

The system shall evaluate:

* Exposure to regulations such as:

  * EU AI Act
  * GDPR
  * HIPAA
  * CCPA
* Whether the use case falls into **prohibited, high-risk, or regulated AI categories**
* Data residency and cross-border processing
* Intellectual property contamination risk from training data
* Explainability requirements for automated decisions
* Liability surface if the AI makes harmful or incorrect decisions

---

### 3.2 Data Risk

The system shall determine:

* Type of data used (PII, PHI, financial, proprietary, public)
* Data ownership and licensing rights
* Whether training data can legally be used for AI training
* Risk of data leakage into model training (especially for SaaS APIs)
* Data lineage and traceability
* Ability to delete or retract data from models
* Sensitivity classification of prompts and outputs

---

### 3.3 Model & Technical Risk

The system shall assess:

* Whether the model is:

  * Deterministic vs probabilistic
  * Explainable vs black-box
* Model drift and update frequency
* Hallucination likelihood for the use case
* Prompt injection and adversarial input risk
* Dependency on external APIs
* Vendor model transparency
* Ability to audit model behavior
* Reproducibility of outputs
* Security surface (prompt attacks, data exfiltration)

---

### 3.4 Vendor / Supply Chain Risk (for Buy/Use)

The system shall evaluate:

* Vendor AI governance maturity
* Model training disclosures
* SOC2 / ISO compliance
* Data usage terms in contracts
* Sub-processors and hidden model providers
* Right to audit
* Vendor lock-in risk
* SLA for model behavior changes
* Versioning guarantees

---

### 3.5 Operational Risk

The system shall determine:

* Human oversight requirements
* Failure modes and blast radius
* Monitoring capability for outputs
* Incident response plan for AI failures
* Rollback capability
* Logging and traceability of AI decisions
* User misuse potential inside the company

---

### 3.6 Ethical & Reputational Risk

The system shall evaluate:

* Bias and discrimination potential
* Content generation risk (harmful, defamatory, unsafe)
* Impact on customers and public perception
* Workforce impact and decision automation risk
* Use in sensitive domains (hiring, lending, healthcare, legal, policing)

---

## 4. System Functional Requirements

### 4.1 Intake Questionnaire Engine

The system shall provide a dynamic questionnaire that adapts based on:

* Industry
* Geography
* AI use case
* Build vs Buy vs Use

---

### 4.2 Risk Scoring Engine

The system shall:

* Assign weighted scores to each risk domain
* Produce:

  * Domain risk scores
  * Composite AI Risk Score
* Allow configurable weighting by organization

---

### 4.3 Use-Case Classification Engine

The system shall map the proposed AI use case to regulatory categories such as those defined in the EU AI Act.

---

### 4.4 Evidence Collection Module

The system shall require upload of:

* Vendor contracts
* Data flow diagrams
* Architecture diagrams
* Security attestations
* Model documentation

And validate completeness before scoring.

---

### 4.5 Continuous Monitoring

The system shall not be a one-time assessment. It must:

* Re-score risk when:

  * Vendor updates model
  * Regulations change
  * Data sources change
  * Use case changes
* Alert stakeholders when risk classification changes

---

### 4.6 Explainable Risk Report Generator

The system shall generate:

* Executive summary
* Legal/compliance report
* Technical/security report
* Required mitigations
* Go / No-Go recommendation

---

## 5. Non-Functional Requirements

* Enterprise grade security (RBAC, audit logs)
* Evidence retention for legal defense
* API access for GRC / compliance tools
* Versioned assessments
* Multi-tenant architecture
* Full audit trail of scoring decisions

---

## 6. Output Artifacts

For each assessment, the system shall produce:

1. AI Risk Certificate
2. Mitigation checklist
3. Required governance controls
4. Contract clause recommendations
5. Monitoring checklist post-deployment

---

## 7. Differentiator (What makes this powerful)

This system is not a questionnaire.

It is a **decision-defense system**.
It answers:

> “If this AI system causes harm, can we prove we acted responsibly before adopting it?”

That is what legal teams, boards, and insurers will care about.
